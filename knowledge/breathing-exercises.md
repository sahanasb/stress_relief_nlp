# Slow Breathing Exercises

## When it helps
Slow breathing can calm the body when stress shows up as a racing heart, tight chest, shaky hands, or a mind that will not slow down. It works well before a presentation, an exam, a hard conversation, or when trying to fall asleep.

## Box breathing
Breathe in gently through the nose for a count of four. Hold for four. Breathe out slowly through the mouth for four. Hold for four. Repeat for about four rounds. If holding the breath feels uncomfortable, skip the holds and just breathe in and out evenly.

## Longer exhale
Breathe in through the nose for a count of four, then breathe out through pursed lips for a count of six. A longer exhale than inhale tends to settle the body. Try five or six slow breaths and notice the shoulders dropping.

## Good to know
Breathe low into the belly rather than high into the chest. If dizziness appears, return to normal breathing. These exercises ease everyday stress but are not a treatment for a medical condition.
