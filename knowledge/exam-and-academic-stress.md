# Exam And Academic Stress

## When it helps
School and university pressure such as exams, assignments, and grades can feel enormous because everything seems due at once. Breaking the pressure into pieces makes it manageable.

## Make the pile smaller
Write down everything that is due and when. Pick the single most urgent item and define the smallest next step, such as opening the notes and reviewing one topic for twenty-five minutes. Work in short focused blocks with five-minute breaks. Active practice like quizzing yourself or explaining a topic out loud works better than rereading.

## The night before
Stop heavy studying in good time, pack what you need, and protect your sleep. A rested brain recalls better than a tired one. A light walk or a short breathing exercise can settle nerves.

## Test nerves
Some nervousness is normal and can sharpen focus. In the exam, read the whole paper, start with questions you can answer, and take a slow breath if your mind blanks. One bad grade does not define your ability.
