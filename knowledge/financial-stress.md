# Financial Stress

## When it helps
Worry about bills, debt, or an uncertain income can occupy the mind all day. Avoiding the numbers usually makes the anxiety larger, while facing them in small steps brings relief.

## Get the picture
Write down what comes in, what goes out, and what is owed. Seeing it on one page often feels less frightening than the vague worry. Sort bills by urgency and handle the most pressing one first.

## Take one step
Choose a single action: call a lender or landlord to ask about a payment plan, cancel one unused subscription, or check what support you may qualify for. Many organisations prefer to arrange a plan than to chase missed payments.

## Good to know
Non-profit credit counselling services and student or community financial offices can offer free guidance. This is general support and not financial advice for your specific situation.
