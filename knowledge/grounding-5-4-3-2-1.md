# Grounding With The 5-4-3-2-1 Exercise

## When it helps
Grounding brings attention out of anxious thoughts and back to the present moment. It is useful when someone feels overwhelmed, panicky, spaced out, or caught in a spiral of what-ifs.

## How to do it
Slowly name five things you can see around you. Then four things you can feel, such as your feet on the floor or the chair against your back. Then three things you can hear. Then two things you can smell, or two scents you like. Finally one thing you can taste, or one slow breath. Go at your own pace and say each one out loud or in your head.

## Simple alternatives
Press both feet into the floor and notice the pressure. Hold a warm mug and feel its warmth. Describe the room in detail as if explaining it to someone who cannot see it.

## Good to know
The aim is not to push feelings away but to steady yourself so you can deal with them. After the exercise, check in with how your body feels.
