# Movement And Body Reset

## When it helps
Stress hormones prepare the body for action. Gentle movement helps burn off that energy and lifts mood, especially after sitting for long periods or when feeling stuck.

## Quick resets
Take a ten-minute walk, ideally outdoors in daylight. Roll the shoulders, stretch the neck gently, and shake out the arms and legs. Drink a glass of water and have a snack if you have not eaten.

## Build the habit
Short daily movement matters more than occasional intense workouts. Pair it with something you enjoy, such as music or a call with a friend. Light exercise earlier in the day can also help sleep.

## Good to know
Choose movement that feels comfortable for your body and stop if something hurts.
