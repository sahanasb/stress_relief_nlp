# Progressive Muscle Relaxation

## When it helps
Stress is often held in the body as tight shoulders, a clenched jaw, or tense hands. Progressive muscle relaxation releases that tension step by step and is helpful in the evening or when feeling wound up and restless.

## How to do it
Sit or lie comfortably. Starting with the hands, make a fist for about five seconds, then let go and notice the difference for ten seconds. Move on to the arms, shoulders, face by scrunching and releasing, then stomach, legs, and feet. Tense gently, never to the point of pain.

## Quick version
If time is short, just do shoulders and jaw. Lift the shoulders toward the ears, hold, and drop them. Then clench the jaw briefly and let it hang loose.

## Good to know
Skip any muscle group that is injured or sore. Practising a few minutes a day makes it work faster when you need it.
