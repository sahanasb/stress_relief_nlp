# Public Speaking And Performance Nerves

## When it helps
Presentations, interviews, auditions, and talks cause racing hearts, dry mouths, and fear of forgetting everything. These sensations are the body preparing for something that matters.

## Prepare and rehearse
Practise out loud, ideally standing, and rehearse the opening until it feels automatic because the start is the hardest part. Keep notes to a few key points rather than a script. If you forget a line, pause and breathe; pauses sound confident to the audience.

## In the moment
A few slow breaths before starting help. Plant your feet, relax your shoulders, and speak a little slower than feels natural. Try reframing nerves as excitement. Focus on what your audience will gain instead of how you look.

## Afterwards
Notice what went well before listing what to improve. Audiences usually notice far less than speakers fear.
