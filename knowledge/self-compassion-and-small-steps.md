# Self-Compassion And Small Steps

## When it helps
When stress piles up, many people become harsh with themselves, which makes it harder to act. Kindness toward yourself is not laziness; it helps you recover and keep going.

## Talk to yourself like a friend
Notice the inner critic and ask what you would say to a friend in the same situation. Acknowledge that this is hard and that struggling is human, then choose a small next move.

## Shrink the task
When something feels too big, agree to work on it for just two minutes. Starting is usually the hardest part. Celebrate small wins, like sending the email or taking the walk, because they build momentum.

## Good to know
Progress is rarely a straight line. A hard day does not erase what you have achieved.
