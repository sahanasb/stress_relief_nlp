# Sleep And Winding Down

## When it helps
Stress and poor sleep feed each other. Lying awake with a busy mind is very common, especially before deadlines or big events.

## Evening wind-down
Keep a regular sleep and wake time. Dim the lights and put screens away for the last half hour. Avoid caffeine late in the day and heavy meals close to bed. A warm shower, light stretching, or slow breathing signals the body that the day is ending.

## Park the worries
Keep a notebook by the bed. Write down tomorrow's tasks and anything that is circling in your mind, then tell yourself it is handled until morning. If you cannot sleep after about twenty minutes, get up, do something quiet and dim, and return when sleepy rather than staring at the clock.

## Good to know
One bad night is not a disaster; the body adapts. If sleep problems last for weeks or affect daily life, it is worth speaking with a doctor.
