# When To Reach Out For More Support

## When it helps
Everyday stress usually eases with rest, practical steps, and talking things through. Some situations call for more support than self-help.

## Signs to reach out
Stress or low mood that lasts for weeks, interferes with sleep, work, study, or relationships, or leads to relying on alcohol or other substances to cope. Panic that keeps returning, or feeling numb and unable to enjoy things, are also good reasons to speak with a doctor, counsellor, or therapist.

## Who can help
A trusted friend or family member, a school or workplace counselling service, a doctor, or a licensed therapist. Asking for help is a strength.

## If safety is a concern
If you ever feel you might hurt yourself, or are in danger, contact local emergency services right away. In the United States you can call or text 988, the Suicide and Crisis Lifeline, at any time.
