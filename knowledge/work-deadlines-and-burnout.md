# Work Stress, Deadlines, And Burnout

## When it helps
Heavy workloads, tight deadlines, difficult colleagues, or feeling constantly behind can leave people exhausted and unable to think straight.

## Brain dump and prioritise
Write everything on your mind onto one list. Mark what is urgent and what is important, and circle just one top priority for today. Ask what can wait, what can be delegated, and what can be dropped. Clear thinking often returns once tasks are outside your head and on paper.

## Protect your energy
Take a real break away from the screen, eat, drink water, and step outside. Set a clear stop time for the day where possible. Saying "I can do A by Friday, but B would need to move" is a reasonable way to discuss capacity with a manager.

## Signs of burnout
Long-lasting exhaustion, dread about work, and feeling detached or ineffective are signs to take seriously. Rest, boundaries, and talking to someone you trust help. If it persists, consider speaking with a doctor or a counsellor.
